Swipe Ready Child Safety Standards

Effective 7 October 2026

Swipe Ready has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page sets out our standards and how to report a concern.

Adults only

  • Swipe Ready is for adults aged 18 and over who use dating apps. Users must confirm they are 18 or older before using the app.
  • The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use it.

What is prohibited

  • Uploading, creating or seeking any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers minors, including CSAM.
  • Using the app to review photos of anyone under 18, or to prepare a dating profile for a minor.
  • Using the app to groom, solicit or exploit minors in any way.

Photos are analysed by Google Gemini, which applies Google's own safety systems and refuses to process prohibited content.

How the app is designed

  • Swipe Ready has no user accounts, no messaging, no profiles and no way to share content with other users. It only reviews photos the user chooses, for the user.
  • Photos go from the user's device directly to Google Gemini for analysis. We do not run servers that receive, store or distribute user photos.

Reporting a concern

  • In the app: tap Report a safety concern at the bottom of the main screen. It opens an email to our child safety contact.
  • By email: flataunir@gmail.com, subject "Child safety concern".
  • We review every report promptly and take appropriate action, including removing access where we can and cooperating with law enforcement.
  • If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Reporting to authorities

When we become aware of CSAM or child exploitation connected to the app, we report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through the CyberTipline and to the relevant regional authorities, as required by applicable law. You can also report directly to NCMEC, or to the INHOPE hotline in your country.

Compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations, and with Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

Child safety point of contact

For questions about these standards or our practices: flataunir@gmail.com.
